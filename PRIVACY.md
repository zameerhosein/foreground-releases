# Foreground privacy policy

Foreground does not collect, send or share any personal data.

Everything the app stores (your books, pages, window positions and settings) is saved only on your
own computer, in the app's data folder. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and the app has no accounts,
analytics or advertising.

The optional browser extension (available in the GitHub version) only talks to the Foreground app
on the same computer, to read which tabs are open. It sends nothing over the internet.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/zameerhosein/foreground-releases
